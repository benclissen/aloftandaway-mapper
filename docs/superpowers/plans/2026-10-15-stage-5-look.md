# Stage 5 look implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking. Do not begin implementation until the user approves this plan.

**Goal:** Videos look on-brand, with the right vehicle on each leg: eleven map styles (two of them Aloft & Away styles), about twenty transport icons in three symbol styles, Plus Jakarta Sans signs and map labels, hill shading with 3D terrain and tilt, and the two camera fixes from the Stage 4 hands-on test.

**Architecture:**
- **Styles:** one style builder in the studio turns a palette and a few switches into a MapLibre style. Satellite is a raster style with the same label layers on top, and terrain adds a raster-dem source, a hillshade layer and `setTerrain`.
- **Vehicles:** drawn on the overlay canvas in the route layer, from icon path data generated from Lucide plus five icons drawn in the same style. Which way an icon faces is decided in `core`, per leg.
- **Credits:** the burned-in and written credit becomes a function of the scene.
- **Data:** the tile proxy gains the EOx satellite and AWS elevation sources, and serves Plus Jakarta Sans glyphs from `data/glyphs`.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Zod 4, MapLibre GL 6, React, Vite, Playwright with Chrome, Vitest, pixelmatch, Lucide (ISC), Plus Jakarta Sans (OFL).

**Spec:** [Mapper design](../specs/2026-10-13-mapper-local-agent-design.md): Project file, Core › Camera and Validation, Studio and server, Rendering (layers) and Testing, and the revisions at the top. Also read `AGENTS.md` and `docs/plan.md`. The decisions made while brainstorming this stage are in "Stage 5 decisions" below; Task 13 copies them into the spec.

## Stage 5 decisions

- **Brand source:** the Aloft & Away design system repo (`~/Documents/Code/Aloft And Away Design System`, local, not part of this repo). It defines coral `#dc8376`, navy `#0a2434`, midnight `#1c1930`, sage `#bcc8a2`, sand `#867a5b`, page grey `#F4F4F4`, Plus Jakarta Sans, and Lucide icons. The palette and font names are public in this repo by the user's choice. Recoleta (the brand display font) is commercial and is not used or shipped.
- **Eleven styles:** `aloftandaway` (the "series map" look, and the default for new projects), `aloftandaway-warm`, `paper`, `light`, `dark`, `nautical`, `atlas`, `outdoor`, `satellite`, `monochrome` and `blank`. The palettes are in Task 4.
- **Symbol styles:** `look.symbolStyle?: 'badge' | 'badge-line' | 'icon'`, default `badge`.
  - `badge` is a navy disc with a white ring and a white icon.
  - `badge-line` is the same disc in the line colour.
  - `icon` is a navy icon on a white halo.
- **Which way icons face:**
  - Side-view icons flip to face their direction. The flip is decided per leg: flipped when the leg's end is west of its start.
  - Plane, helicopter and arrow turn with the line.
  - Front-view icons stay upright.
- **Signs:** only the existing `tag` style. Its font becomes Plus Jakarta Sans 600, in every style.
- **Map labels:** Plus Jakarta Sans in the two Aloft & Away styles, from glyphs built once and committed. Every other style keeps OpenFreeMap's Noto Sans.
- **New project defaults:** theme `aloftandaway`, line colour `#dc8376`, `symbolStyle` left unset (so `badge`).
- **Terrain:** `look.terrain: true` turns on hill shading plus 3D elevation (exaggeration 1.3) from AWS Terrain Tiles (Terrarium encoding).
- **Tilt:** shot `pitch` (0–60, unchanged) and a new optional `camera.follow.pitch`.
- **Follow fields:** every `camera.follow` field becomes optional, filled from defaults.
- **Camera fixes from the Stage 4 test:**
  - The default glide opens on the first leg and pulls out to the whole route.
  - Follow defaults become `lookAhead 0.25`, `smoothing 0.2`.
  - The aim point is capped to a quarter of the frame.
  - The heading-up bearing is smoothed three times more slowly than the position.
- **Still parked:** the flight bow size and the Baltic skerries.

## Global constraints

- Coordinates are `[lng, lat]` (GeoJSON order) everywhere in the public interface.
- `core` imports nothing else from the package and has no DOM, filesystem or network imports.
- Every render carries the map credit for what it shows. Satellite uses EOxCloudless **2016** only (CC BY 4.0); later years are non-commercial and must not be used.
- **Clean-room:** no Map Animator code, themes, icons, sign art, UI text or file format. **Public repo:** nothing private. Lucide is ISC and Plus Jakarta Sans is OFL; ship their licence texts. Recoleta must not be committed.
- **Project files:** stay schema version 2. New fields are optional, so every existing version 1 and version 2 file still loads unchanged.
- Every camera state is a pure function of the frame number.
- Defaults for new projects: 3840×2160, 25 fps, 16:9.
- Downloads are untrusted data. Build scripts write them into a new, empty temp folder and only read them as data.
- Run `npm test` and `npm run test:e2e` one after the other, never together. CLI unit tests rebuild `dist`.

## Review focus

1. **Old projects.** A version 1 file, and a version 2 file with `theme: 'paper'`, the old line colour and no `symbolStyle`, must load and render as before (except the sign font). Test: Task 1, "loads a stage 4 project unchanged".
2. **Burned-in credit per style.** A satellite scene with terrain must burn in all three credit lines and fit at 1080p. A `credit: 'description'` render must write the same text to the FCPXML notes and the manifest. Test: Task 2, "credit for satellite with terrain", and Task 12, "burned credit fits at 1080p".
3. **Terrain and the layered export.** With terrain on, the route layer's line must sit exactly over the map layer's terrain. The spec's composite check (the layers composited against the `all` layer) must still pass. Test: Task 8, "route layer drapes on terrain", in `layers.test.ts` (network-gated).
4. **Icon flipping.** On a loop route (east, then back west), the car flips once per leg and never mid-leg. A leg that ends due north or south of where it starts doesn't flip. Test: Task 6, "flips per leg, not per frame".
5. **Offline and failing sources.** With no network, `blank` still renders. A satellite or terrain tile that fails after its retries gives the same clear map-data error as a basemap tile does today. Test: Task 5, "elevation tile failure surfaces as a map-data error".

### Task 7: Drawing the vehicle and the Tag in Plus Jakarta Sans

**Model:** Sonnet 5.5 (`sonnet`).

**Files:**

**The vehicle:** drawn in the route layer (and `all`) at `state.head.at`, for the leg's `effectiveSymbol`.

**Sizes (layout px):**
- The badge radius is 22 and the white ring 3.
- The icon is 26 units across, with a stroke of 2.2 in icon units, scaled.
- `icon` style: a white halo stroke of 6, then a navy `#0a2434` stroke on top.

**Colours:**
- `badge`: navy `#0a2434` disc, white icon.
- `badge-line`: a `look.line.color` disc, white icon.
- `dot`: the old head look (a line-colour circle, radius `max(5, width × 1.5)`, white ring 2), whatever the style.
- `none`: nothing.

**Orientation:**
- `side` icons mirror when `legFlipped`.
- `turn` icons rotate by the head's heading minus the map bearing, plus a per-icon offset from its `facing` (`north-east` −45°, `up` 0°).
